# Mathematics Roadmap for AI & ML Engineering

A practical mathematics roadmap for becoming an **AI Engineer / ML Engineer**.

The goal is not to study mathematics for its own sake, but to learn the mathematics that is actually useful for:

* Machine Learning
* Deep Learning
* Neural Networks
* Optimization
* Statistics
* Probability
* Computer Vision
* NLP
* Reinforcement Learning

# 13. Recommended Learning Order

A practical order is:

```text
Basic Algebra
      ↓
Linear Algebra
      ↓
Calculus
      ↓
Probability
      ↓
Statistics
      ↓
Optimization
      ↓
Information Theory
      ↓
ML Mathematics
      ↓
Deep Learning Mathematics
      ↓
Advanced AI Mathematics
```

You don't need to completely finish one mathematical field before touching ML.

A better approach is:

```text
Learn Concept
     ↓
Understand Mathematical Idea
     ↓
See ML Application
     ↓
Implement It
     ↓
Continue
```

## Core Goal

You do **not** need to become a mathematician.

You need enough mathematics to understand:

```text
Why the algorithm works
        ↓
What the equations mean
        ↓
How the model learns
        ↓
How to debug its behavior
        ↓
How to implement or modify it
```

> **For AI/ML engineering, mathematics is a tool for understanding models—not a separate destination.**
